Frequently asked questions

You've got questions. We've got answers.

Do I need a nursing or medical background?
No. You don't need to be a nurse to become a sleep consultant. You need training from people who are. Our students are stay-at-home moms, nannies, doulas and teachers. You bring the care, we bring the medical backbone.
I'm busy. Will I have time to finish?
It's self-paced and 100% online, with lifetime access. Most students finish in 4 to 6 weeks, studying during naptime or in the pockets of time they have. No deadlines and no cohort to keep up with.
How much does it cost?
We offer flexible payment plans as low as $140 a month. We'll walk you through the exact options on your free call, so you can pick what fits your budget.
Isn't the market already saturated?
About 130 million babies are born every year. You don't need every family, just a handful. Many of our top grads barely post online and are fully booked through referrals.
What happens on the free career call?
It's a free 30-minute call with our team. Please show up on time with your camera on, and reschedule if something comes up. We look at where you're starting from and give you an honest read on whether IPSP is the right fit. It's a real conversation, not a sales pitch.
