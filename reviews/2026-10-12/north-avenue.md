# LPCR page review: North Avenue Education (Scott Clyburn)

Review date: 2026-10-12. Read-only. Nothing was changed in Meta, GHL, Drive or the sheet.

## Verdict

The ad sells a call and the page sells a one-pager. The ad's primary text says "Book a Free Academic Game Plan Call" twice and its pain is "juggling a different tutor for every subject". The page headline talks about "Tutoring Packages", the offer is a "FREE ONE-PAGER", and the button says "Get Started >>". On mobile the form is below the first screen. LPCR is 10.6% (last 7 days), about half the 20% goal.

## Fix first (not tests)

1. **The ad's offer doesn't match the page.** Ad text: "📞 Book a Free Academic Game Plan Call." and "⬇️ Book your Free Academic Game Plan Call." Page: "FREE ONE-PAGER / Get Instant Access". The sheet's "LP A/B test" tab shows the one-pager beat the call ("WINNER: Free One-Pager, Get Instant Access / LOSER: Free Call, Book Now"). The page was updated but the ad text still promises the call. Only the ad image matches ("Tap to get the free breakdown of how it all works."). Ema's call on the ad copy. Until then, the page should at least name the same freebie the image does (see T1).
2. **Old SAT class sitelink on the live ad.** The P2 ad preview carries sitelinks Meta added: "See more", "Apply today", "Register today", "Join us today", and "SAT 3/30 Class", which points to `northaveeducation.com/classes/sat-intensive-q22025sati1?utm_medium=cpc&utm_source=google-ads`. That is a past class from a Google Ads link. Playbook: "every AI/enhancement off". Those clicks count as link clicks but never reach the opt-in, which drags LPCR down. Ask Ema to turn site links off (needs approval, I did not change anything).
3. **Button text is a generic "Get Started >>".** The page's own outer button says "GET YOUR FREE ONE-PAGER" but the form button inside says "Get Started >>". Playbook (process step 4): button says what they get, not "Get Started". Easy fix, also in T3.
4. **Guarantee wording differs from the client's terms.** Ad: "If the tutor match isn't right within 3 sessions, we rematch and give you a free session." Profile: "If your first tutor match isn't an expert, let us know and we'll repair and give you the session for free." The "within 3 sessions" limit is not in the profile. Confirm with Scott. The page has no guarantee at all.
5. **The "$600+ a Month" number on the live headline B has no source.** Mobile served "Most Families Pay 3 Separate Tutors $600+ a Month." That figure isn't in the profile or the ad. Confirm with Scott or swap it out (T1).
6. **Reviews heading talks to students.** "What Do Students Love About North Avenue Education?" The buyer is the parent ("Parents - the students are below 18 years old"). Change to "What Do Parents Say About North Avenue Education?"

## How to run them

- Traffic: 104 link clicks in the last 7 days (sheet), about 15 a day.
- One test needs about 150 visitors per version, so 300 per test. That is about 3 weeks per test at today's traffic. Five separate tests would take about 15 weeks. Too slow.
- Bundle into 2 rounds of A vs B:
  - **Round 1 (weeks 1-3), first screen:** T1 + T2 together. Version B = new headline + one-line call-out + form in the first mobile screen. Clarity labels **T1-A / T1-B**.
  - **Round 2 (weeks 4-6), form area:** T3 + T4 + T5 together on top of the Round 1 winner. Clarity labels **T2-A / T2-B**.
- Call it the way the sheet already says: at 150+ visits per version, a lead of 3+ percentage points wins. Closer than that, run another week.
- The current headline test started 10/9 (about 38 clicks so far). If the "$600+" can't be confirmed, stop it and start Round 1 instead.
- Do the Fix first items before Round 1 so they don't muddy the results (sitelinks off, button text, reviews heading).
